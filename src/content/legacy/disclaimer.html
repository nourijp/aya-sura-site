About the Illustrations

Please be assured that the Aya & Sura flashcards have not included any illustrations of Allah (S.W.T.) or any of the Holy Prophets (A.S).

About the Quranic Verses

For each Quranic word taught in the Aya & Sura flashcards, a Quranic verse has been provided; however, the illustration on the opposite side of each flashcard is intended to help teach the meaning of the word, but does not necessary represent the exact meaning within the Quranic verse.

Concerning Respect for the Quranic Words and Verses

In order to respect the Quranic words and verses used within the Aya & Sura flashcards, please use the cards on clean surfaces and be respectful when dealing with the cards. It is highly recommended to do ablution (wudhoo) when handling the cards. [Parents, please teach your children the above mentioned etiquette for playing with the cards. In this way, Insha’Allah the children will learn to respect and love the Holy Quran.]
